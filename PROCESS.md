## 5. Remux / chip-away

Fairy Tale / Equals lessons applied:

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Short clips · fast select | Long mushy takes you “fix in grade” |
| Chip-away — fix only broken moments | Whole-timeline regen for local flaws |
| Mood-match to lyric energy | Lip-sync theater |
| Hard cuts · trim dead tails | Freeze pads to fake length |
| Prefer unused takes | Stretch a bad clip |

Typical normalize: 1280×720, 30 fps, silent picture bed → mux master audio → HQ encode for YouTube.
